New in 1.19.0:
- "When you study" on the Stats page now shows the half hour you study in most often, like "You study most between 7:30 PM and 8:00 PM", instead of an average time. That half hour is highlighted on the clock.
- The "Today" total at the top of the timer now counts up while the timer runs, with a small dot while you're studying. If you cancel a session, that time drops back off.
- Fixed friends showing as online or offline when they were actually studying (or the other way round). Online and studying now always agree, they're worked out from the server's clock so a PC clock that's a few minutes off no longer throws them out, and friends stop seeing you as studying as soon as you close the app.
