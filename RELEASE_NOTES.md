New in 1.6.0:
- A startup animation: the app loads, the logo builds itself, then flies into the screen.
- A welcome screen every time you open the app: hills full of trees you can unlock, a glowing mythic tree far in the distance, the sky follows the time of day, and your stats, next test and this week at a glance. Turn it off in Settings if you like.
- Grades use your school's bands: E, E+, D, D+, C, C+, B, B+, A, A+.
- "What do I need?" is now just a percentage box, with the matching letter grade next to it.
- Change any subject's colour in Settings (18 colours or pick any colour).
