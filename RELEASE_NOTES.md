New in 1.9.0:
- Friends tab: everyone gets a 4-letter friend code. Add friends to see their stats, study sessions, trees and badges, and send them coins or cases as gifts.
- Friend streaks: study at least 15 minutes a day, both of you, to grow a streak. It pays out coins and free cases as it grows.
- Weekly team tasks: three new tasks with each friend every week. Each of you has to do your share.
- New Friends badges for streaks and team tasks.
- Your grades stay private unless you switch on sharing.
- The sidebar now shows just the Timer. Press the arrow under it to open the other tabs.
