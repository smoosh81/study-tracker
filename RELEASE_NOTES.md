New in 1.18.0:
- See a friend's full stats: open their profile and press "See all of their stats" to get the same Stats page you have, with days, weeks, months, their forest and when they usually study. Click any of their subjects to open that subject's own page.
- Live status: friends now show as online, offline, on a break, or studying, with what and for how long, like "Studying · 34 min of Accounting".
- Real-time numbers: the leaderboard, friend cards and today's time keep up while friends study, instead of waiting until they finish. If a friend cancels a session, that time drops straight back off.
- Global leaderboard: switch the weekly leaderboard to "Everyone" to see how you rank against everyone using the app. It only shows names, pictures and hours. You can hide yourself from it under Privacy.
- Profile pictures: click your picture on the Friends tab and pick any tree you've unlocked.
- Hours on the global leaderboard can't grow faster than real time, so nobody can jump to the top with a fake number.
