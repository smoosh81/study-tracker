New in 1.20.0:
- You can now sell trees you don't want. Hover a tree on the Trees page and press Sell.
- A tree sells for 40% of the cheapest chest that can drop it: Common, Uncommon and Rare trees sell for 20 coins, Epic for 60, and Legendary and Mythical for 160.
- If you have more than one copy, pick how many to sell, or sell them all at once.
- You always keep one Oak and one Pine, and you can't sell the last copy of the tree that's growing right now.
- Selling your last copy takes it off your favourites, and your next session grows an Oak instead if it was picked.
- Your study time, sessions, subjects, grades and garden aren't touched.
