New in 1.19.2:
- Chest do-over: you can hand back up to 3 trees you got from chests before this update. On the Trees page, pick a tree and say which chest it came from (only chests that can drop it are shown). If you paid coins you get that chest's price back, and if it was a free chest you get the chest back to open again. This is a one-time offer.
- Every chest you open is now remembered (which chest, which tree and when), so we can always see exactly what came from where.
- Your study time, sessions, subjects and grades aren't touched by any of this.
