New in 1.11.0:
- Letter grades: on the Grades page, press the A+ button next to a score to enter a letter instead of a %. Handy for exams that only give a letter.
- Averages count a letter as the middle of its band, so a B (70 to 74%) counts as 72% and an A+ counts as 95%.
