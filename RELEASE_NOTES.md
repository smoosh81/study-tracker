New in 1.22.0:
- Fixed trees twitching when you hover over them in the garden. Checking a tree's stats or picking one up to move it is smooth now.
- Garden designer: press "Design garden" on the Garden page. Clear the whole garden into a tray on the side, then drag trees and decorations back in exactly where you want them. Click something in the tray to drop it in, or drag a tree back onto the tray to put it away.
- Nothing is ever deleted: trees and decorations you clear just wait in the tray. "Plant the rest" puts them all back, and "Auto-arrange" lets the garden lay itself out again.
- New trees still plant themselves automatically. If you'd rather place every tree yourself, turn off "Plant new trees automatically" in Settings or in the designer, and new trees will wait in the tray.
- Focused mode: turn it on in Settings and the timer goes full screen with just the clock and your tree while you study. Press Esc to leave it.
- Choose which tabs show in the sidebar. Go to Settings, then Sidebar tabs, and click a tab to hide it or bring it back. Timer and Settings always stay.
- Your study time, sessions, subjects, grades and collection aren't touched.
