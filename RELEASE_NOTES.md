New in 1.8.0:
- Longer sessions grow more trees: one for every half hour, always a whole number (an hour grows 2, two hours grow 4, up to 12). Your past sessions have been regrown to match.
- A session's trees are planted side by side. Hover any of them to light up the whole group and see that session's totals.
- New Garden shop on the Garden tab: spend coins on decorations like flower beds, lanterns, a pond, a fountain and a windmill. They're just for looks and don't change your timer or the coins you earn.
